Data Center Tracker: Alabama and Georgia

This map tracks data center developments and related policy actions across Alabama and Georgia. Our dataset combines facility records from Compute Atlas (CC-BY-4.0) with original research including public filings, zoning records, regulatory approvals, and local news coverage.

Each dot represents a project or policy action. The color indicates its current phase:

Proposed — announced, filed, or under review
Approved — zoning or permits granted, not yet building
Active — under construction
Operating — in service
Stopped — denied, withdrawn, paused, or moratorium
Policy — ordinance or regulatory action

Click any marker to see the project name, developer, location, regulatory body, capacity and investment figures (where available), a timeline of key milestones, and links to original news sources.
