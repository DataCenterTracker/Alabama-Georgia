Data Center Tracker: Alabama and Georgia

This map tracks proposed, permitted, and active data center developments across Alabama and Georgia. Our dataset combines facility records from Compute Atlas (an open, CC-BY-4.0 licensed database) with original research by our team, including public filings, zoning records, regulatory approvals, and local news coverage.

Each dot on the map represents a single data center project. The color indicates its current stage in the development pipeline:

Proposed — announced, filed, or land acquired; no approval yet
Permitted — zoning or incentive approval granted
Under Construction — active building confirmed by sources
Operational — facility is live and serving workloads
Stalled / Paused — development stopped or on hold

Click any marker to see the project name, developer, location, regulatory body, capacity and investment figures (where available), a timeline of key milestones, and links to original news sources.
